Inside a modal, everything goes behind the Filter button.

## Right side: View + Actions

Ordered left to right:

1. **Active multi-select**: "N Rows Selected · Clear", when <code>selectionSummary</code> is set and rows are selected.
2. **View mode toggle**: a button group, passed as <code>viewModeToggle</code>, that switches between the table's views, e.g. Cost / Price. Mark the current view <code>active</code>.
3. **Column selector**: when any column has <code>canHide</code>.
4. **View selector**: List / Card, with <code>withCardView</code>.
5. **Actions**: <code>actions</code>, typically the overflow menu.

## Responsive display

When there is less than **40px** between the left and right sides, the controls switch to their mobile format, even on a desktop-width screen. They always use it below the `sm` breakpoint. With nothing on the right side, there's no gap to measure, so only the `sm` breakpoint applies.

In the mobile format:

- Search and Filter become icon buttons. Search opens a full-width field below the bar.
- Group By is grouped with the filters, and shows once the filter button is tapped.
- The selection summary shows just the count and an ×.
- The column and view selectors become icon buttons.
- The view mode toggle moves to the top of the column selector's menu. It stays in the bar when it's the only item on the right side.

See the **Responsive Display** story, and drag its corner to watch the switch.
