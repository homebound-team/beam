`BaseCard` is the shared card shell: border, radius, a 184px hero image, and hover and focus states. You supply the body, usually a `CardBody` for the standard eyebrow, title, data list and progress bar. Use `LinkCard` for a short summary card that links somewhere. `Card` is deprecated in favor of `BaseCard`. For a table shown as cards, use `GridTable` with `as="card"`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- `onClick` as a URL makes the hero and body a link; as a function, a button. Either way the card gets a pointer and a hover shadow.
- `action` overlays an `IconButton` (or a `ButtonMenu`, when it has `items`) on the hero's top-right corner, and `tag` overlays a status `Tag` on its top-left. Both sit outside the card's link, so they act on their own.
- `footer` also sits outside the link, so it can hold its own buttons.
- `children` are already inset, so pass bare content, not a padded wrapper.
- `height` fixes the card's height; the body takes the spare room. Without it, the card sizes to its content.
- An empty `imgAlt` hides the image from screen readers.

## How to use

```tsx
<BaseCard imgSrc={plan.imageUrl} imgAlt="" onClick={`/plans/${plan.id}`} tag={{ text: "Draft", type: "info" }}>
  <CardBody title={plan.name} />
</BaseCard>
```
