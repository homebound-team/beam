`CountBadge` shows a number in a small circle, such as unread comments or active filters.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- It's 16px, and grows to 18px for counts over 100.
- It defaults to white text on blue. `hideIfZero` renders nothing for a count of 0.

## How to use

```tsx
<CountBadge count={unreadCount} hideIfZero />
```
