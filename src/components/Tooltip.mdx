`Tooltip` shows a short hint in a floating bubble while its children are hovered or focused. Use it for extra context that isn't needed to finish a task. Anything the user must read belongs on the page.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- Only one tooltip shows at a time. If one is already open, the next opens immediately and skips its `delay`.
- `placement` defaults to `auto`, which picks the side with the most room and flips near the viewport edge.
- Moving the pointer from the trigger onto the bubble keeps it open, so its content can be selected.

## How to use

Most Beam components already take a `tooltip` prop, and a `disabled` reason shows as a tooltip, so you rarely need `Tooltip` directly:

```tsx
<Button label="Delete" disabled="Only drafts can be deleted." onClick={remove} />
<TextField label="Name" value={name} onChange={setName} tooltip="Shown to homeowners." />
```

Wrap your own content when no prop fits:

```tsx
<Tooltip title="Last synced 5 minutes ago">
  <Icon icon="refresh" />
</Tooltip>
```
