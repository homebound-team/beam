# Carousel

`Carousel` is a horizontally scrolling strip of items, such as cards or thumbnails.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- The back and forward chevrons only appear when there's more to scroll that way, and the items fade out under them.
- A chevron scrolls by the strip's visible width, and items snap into place, so none is left half-shown.
- Each child is one item, and keeps its own width.

## How to use

```tsx
<Carousel gap={16}>
  {photos.map((p) => (
    <img key={p.id} src={p.url} alt={p.caption} width={240} />
  ))}
</Carousel>
```
