# AI Progress Button

`AiProgressButton` starts AI work from a compact button. It rests as the AI star, and its label slides out on hover or keyboard focus. While the work runs, `inProgress` spins the star. For a regular labeled AI action, use `Button` with `variant="ai"`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- On touch screens, and on screens 1024px wide or narrower, the label always shows.
- A string `onClick` renders a link, like `Button`.
- `disabled` can be a reason (any `ReactNode`), shown in a tooltip below the button. `tooltip` shows there too.
- `size` is `sm` (32px), `md` (40px, the default) or `lg` (48px).

## How to use

```tsx
<AiProgressButton label="Generate summary" inProgress={isGenerating} onClick={generateSummary} />
```
