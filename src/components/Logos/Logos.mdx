Beam has three logo components: `HomeboundLogo` (the roofline mark), `BeamLogo`, and `BlueprintAiLogo`.

<ArgTypes of={BeamLogo} />

## Behaviors

- Set either `width` or `height`, in 8px increments, and the other follows the logo's aspect ratio. Passing both is a type error.
- `HomeboundLogo` and `BeamLogo` take a `fill` that defaults to `currentColor`. `BlueprintAiLogo` is always drawn in the brand gradient and has no `fill`.

## How to use

```tsx
<HomeboundLogo height={4} />
<BlueprintAiLogo width={10} />
```
