# AI Slim Banner

`AiSlimBanner` is a one-line AI notice that sits inline, directly above the content it's about, such as a table or form section. It shows the AI star, a title, and an optional text action. For a callout with a message and buttons, use `AiBanner`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- `action` renders as a `text` button at the end of the row.
- A long title wraps, and the star stays aligned with its first line.
- Inside a modal, put it in `ModalBanner` so it spans the modal's full width under the header.

## How to use

```tsx
<AiSlimBanner
  title="Quantities were filled in from the uploaded takeoff."
  action={{ label: "View source", onClick: openSource }}
/>
```
