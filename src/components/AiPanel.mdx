`AiPanel` paints the AI background behind its children, and `AiCard` places a white card under the Blueprint AI logo. They are an escape hatch for one-off AI chrome. Reach for a pre-composed AI component first: `AiBanner`, `AiSlimBanner`, `AiLinkCardGroup`, `AiLoadingPanel`, or `aiMode` on `FormSectionLayout`, `StepperLayout`, `FocusedFormLayout` or a modal. If none of those fit, ask the Design System team whether a component exists or should. The full rule is in `docs/layouts.md`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- `padding` is `sm` (24px across, 16px tall) by default, or `lg` (24px all around).
- `rounded` rounds the corners, for a panel inside page content rather than spanning it.
- Both are capped to the visible content column, so a page widened by an open right pane doesn't stretch them.
- `AiCard` fills its parent by default. `fullWidth={false}` sizes it to its content and centers it. Its `size` sets the logo height and the gap above the card.

## How to use

```tsx
<AiPanel rounded>
  <AiCard size="sm">
    <div css={Css.p2.$}>…</div>
  </AiCard>
</AiPanel>
```
