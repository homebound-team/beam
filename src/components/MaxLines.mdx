# Max Lines

`MaxLines` cuts long content to `maxLines` lines, with a "Show More" / "Show Less" toggle, e.g. for notes or descriptions.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- The toggle only appears when the content overflows, and the check reruns when the content or its width changes.
- New `children` collapse it again.

## How to use

```tsx
<MaxLines maxLines={3}>{project.description}</MaxLines>
```
