`Tag` shows a status, such as Draft, Approved or Overdue. Use `ContextTag` to say what something is, `TagGroup` for a list of labels, and `Chip` for a neutral label.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- `type` sets the color and defaults to `neutral`. `strikethrough` dims the tag and strikes through its text.
- `variant` defaults to `primary`, a tinted uppercase tag. `bold` is a darker fill with white text. `secondary` is outlined, and is what `TagGroup` uses.
- Text is cut to one line. When it's cut, the full text shows in a tooltip, unless `preventTooltip` is set.
- `iconOnly` shows just the `icon`. `text` is still required: screen readers read it, and it shows in the tooltip.

## How to use

```tsx
<Tag text="Approved" type="success" />
<Tag text="Overdue" type="error" icon="error" />
<Tag text="Suggested by Blueprint AI" type="ai" icon="aiStar" iconOnly />
```
