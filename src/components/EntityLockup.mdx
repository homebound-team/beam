# Entity Lockup

<p className="sbdocs-figma">
  <a href="https://www.figma.com/design/givQaQnS8wqXaD89AnBkcR/Guided-Change-Event-Workflow?node-id=2324-7685">

    Entity Lockup
  </a>
</p>

`EntityLockup` identifies an entity, such as a product or option, with an image thumbnail beside its title and description. Use `compact` in dense lists like sidebars. Put trailing details, like a `Tag` and a price, in `right`, and a brand above the title in `eyebrow`.
