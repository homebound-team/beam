`Switch` turns a setting on or off, usually taking effect right away. For a choice that's submitted with a form, a `Checkbox` is often clearer. In a form, use `BoundSwitchField`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- `disabled` can be a reason (any `ReactNode`), shown in a tooltip.
- `withIcon` shows a check in the knob while it's on, and an `x` while it's off.
- `compact` renders a smaller switch.

## How to use

```tsx
<Switch label="Show archived" selected={showArchived} onChange={setShowArchived} />
```
