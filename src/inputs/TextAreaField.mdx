`TextAreaField` is a multi-line text input that grows with its content. Use `TextField` for a single line, and `RichTextField` for formatting or mentions. In a form, use `BoundTextAreaField`.

<ArgTypes />

## Behaviors

- The field grows to fit its text. `maxLines` caps the height, after which it scrolls.
- `preventNewLines` blocks Enter and replaces pasted line breaks with spaces. With it set, Enter runs `onEnter` and blurs the field.
- It shares `TextField`'s field props: `onChange` receives `undefined` when emptied, and `disabled` and `readOnly` can be reasons shown in a tooltip.

## How to use

```tsx
<TextAreaField label="Notes" value={notes} onChange={setNotes} maxLines={8} />
```
