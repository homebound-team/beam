## Usage

Use `MenuTreeSelectField` when a filter accepts several values from nested options. Each option may have `children`. It takes `values` and calls `onSelect({ all, leaf, root })`, the same response as `TreeSelectField`:

- `all` — every selected option, including parents whose children are all selected.
- `leaf` — only selected options without children.
- `root` — only the highest selected options, so a fully selected group reports just its parent.

Selecting a parent selects its enabled children, and selecting every child selects the parent. A partly selected parent shows an indeterminate checkbox.
`values` can hold any of the three shapes; A parent `id` in the `values` array assumes all of its descendants are also selected, whether they are included in `values` or not.
The same value may sit under more than one parent, and selecting it selects every copy.

`selectionDisplay` picks which selections the count badge, tooltip, and open-menu pills include, and defaults to `all`.

Groups start collapsed, except groups holding a selection, so every selected option is visible when the menu opens. `defaultExpanded` starts every group open instead. With a row highlighted, Right Arrow expands a group and then steps into it, and Left Arrow collapses a group or steps out to its parent. Searching shows each match with its ancestors and descendants. Labels wrap instead of truncating.

Plug into `@homebound/form-state` with `BoundMenuTreeSelectField`, which stores `all.values` unless `onSelect` is passed.
