## Usage

Use `MenuMultiSelectField` when a filter accepts several values. It takes `values` and calls `onSelect(values, options)`. Each row has a checkbox, the menu stays open while options are toggled, and the closed field shows in-field pills and a count badge by default. `selectionView` changes that: `badgeOnly` shows only the count badge, and `selectionList` lists them as pills below the field. Use `inField` for short labels and `selectionList` for longer or composite labels. A menu longer than `searchOptionLimit` includes a search field. A selection hidden by the current search stays selected. Pass `onSearch` when the list is too large to hold: the menu lists the `options` the caller returns. A selection the field has already shown stays a pill, and in the count, when that list omits it.

The open menu lists the current selection as pills above the search field. The pills stay on one line and scroll sideways inside the menu; expand them to wrap, up to 40% of the menu height, with the row scrolling on its own. Click a pill to clear that option. The menu stays as wide as the field.

Plug into `@homebound/form-state` with `BoundMenuMultiSelectField`.
